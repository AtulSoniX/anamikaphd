My research focuses on optimization methods for reliable, flexible, and renewable-rich power systems. I work across planning and operation problems where uncertainty, variability, and scale make energy-system decision-making challenging.

## Current Focus Areas

### Robust Power System Planning

Developing optimization methods for reliable and efficient grid planning under uncertainty.

### Renewable Integration

Modeling renewable variability and operational flexibility for modern power systems.

### Stochastic and Convex Optimization

Designing tractable methods for complex power system planning and operation problems.

### Large-Scale Energy Modeling

Building MATLAB and Python workflows for scalable analysis of energy systems.
